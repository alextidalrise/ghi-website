These six came to us between March and June, and four of them have not been listed publicly. Frontline means different things from one resort to the next. At Los Naranjos it can mean a garden that opens onto the ninth. At La Zagaleta it more often means a view across a valley you do not own and cannot build on, which is the more durable thing to buy. Our note on how to read a frontline listing covers the distinction properly. View the collection Ask about a specific house Every property we list has been visited. If you want an honest view on one of them, including the ones we decided not to take on, write back to this address and we will tell you what we saw. Arrange a conversation Before you travel Viewings in July fill up by the middle of June, and most of these houses are shown by appointment. If you would like to see two or three on one trip, tell us early which ones matter to you. The drive between Sotogrande and Marbella East is longer than the map suggests. Written from the Marbella office. Reply to this message and it reaches a person, not a queue.
