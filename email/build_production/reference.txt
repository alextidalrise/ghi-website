SIX HOUSES ON THE FAIRWAY
-------------------------

Frontline property changes hands quietly here. These six came to us
between March and June, and four of them have not been listed publicly.

Frontline means different things from one resort to the next. At Los
Naranjos it can mean a garden that opens onto the ninth. At La Zagaleta
it more often means a view across a valley you do not own and cannot
build on, which is the more durable thing to buy. Our note on how to
read a frontline listing
(https://www.golfhomesinternational.com/guides/reading-a-frontline-listing?utm_source=mailchimp&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=andalucia-frontline)
covers the distinction properly.
