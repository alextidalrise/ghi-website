Four Algarve golf-property destinations

Vilamoura Marina and coastline, Algarve

Vilamoura

Vilamoura brings together marina life, sandy beaches, restaurants, golf and established resort services in one well-connected Algarve destination. Its property offering spans contemporary apartments, residences, houses and villas across several parts of the resort.
See Vilamoura properties
Quinta do Lago and the Ria Formosa, Algarve

Quinta do Lago

Quinta do Lago and the neighbouring Golden Triangle combine established golf, privacy and high-quality residential living beside the protected Ria Formosa landscape. The setting brings together space, managed services and a long-established Algarve residential environment.
See Quinta do Lago properties
Monte Rei Golf & Country Club, eastern Algarve

Monte Rei

Monte Rei offers a private, landscape-led resort environment in the eastern Algarve, centred on golf, space and understated country-club living. The residential collection includes apartments, duplex penthouses, linked villas and plots within the resort.
See Monte Rei properties
Palmares Golf Course and the Atlantic coastline, western Algarve

Palmares

Palmares combines an Atlantic-facing golf setting with convenient access to Lagos and the western Algarve coastline. The residential offering includes residences, apartments and plots within this distinctive coastal resort.
See Palmares properties
