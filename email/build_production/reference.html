Andalucía

Six houses on the fairway

Frontline property changes hands quietly here. These six came to us between March and June, and four of them have not been listed publicly.
Frontline means different things from one resort to the next. At Los Naranjos it can mean a garden that opens onto the ninth. At La Zagaleta it more often means a view across a valley you do not own and cannot build on, which is the more durable thing to buy. Our note on how to read a frontline listing covers the distinction properly.

Before you travel

Viewings in July fill up by the middle of June, and most of these houses are shown by appointment. If you would like to see two or three on one trip, tell us early which ones matter to you. The drive between Sotogrande and Marbella East is longer than the map suggests.
